HOW VERIFICATION WORKS

Each story is matched across independent outlets — international wires (Reuters, AP, AFP), regional press (Hindustan Times, Times of India, The Guardian, Al Jazeera), and official channels (ministries, PIB, WHO). A story only publishes once enough independent sources agree on the core facts. Contradictions are shown, not hidden.
